## 🧭 &nbsp;$ whoami

> *Software Engineering student (Cybersecurity major) at **U-TAD**, operating in the world of **offensive security**: Red Team, pentesting and digital forensics. My mission is to understand systems deeply enough to break them — and then secure them.*

**Reverse engineering is my signature** — from full **Active Directory** intrusions to binary analysis and game hacking *(cheats / anti-cheats)*, which is where it all started. I also build **offensive hardware** (ESP32, Bluetooth/RF, a homemade Flipper Zero). I believe that to truly secure a system, you must first know exactly how to break it.
